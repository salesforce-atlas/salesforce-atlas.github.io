    Interview Prep Checklist¶

    Before Interview¶

    • [ ] Research company and interviewers
    • [ ] Review job description
    • [ ] Practice STAR stories
    • [ ] Review technical topics
    • [ ] Prepare questions to ask
    • [ ] Test video setup (if remote)
    • [ ] Plan professional attire

    Day Of¶

    • [ ] Arrive 10-15 minutes early
    • [ ] Bring notebook and pen
    • [ ] Have certifications ready to discuss
    • [ ] Positive mindset
    • [ ] Ready to ask questions

    After Interview¶

    • [ ] Send thank-you email within 24 hours
    • [ ] Note what went well
    • [ ] Note areas for improvement
    • [ ] Follow up if no response in 1 week

    Quick Tips¶

    Answer Structure (STAR)¶

    • Situation: Set context
    • Task: The challenge
    • Action: What you did
    • Result: The outcome

    Common Interview Mistakes¶

    • ❌ Not researching the company
    • ❌ Rambling answers
    • ❌ Not asking questions
    • ❌ Being negative about past roles
    • ❌ Lying or exaggerating

    Interview Best Practices¶

    • ✅ Be specific with examples
    • ✅ Show enthusiasm
    • ✅ Ask clarifying questions
    • ✅ Admit when you don't know
    • ✅ Explain your thought process
    • ✅ Follow up afterward
