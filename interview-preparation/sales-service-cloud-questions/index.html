    📊 Sales Cloud Fundamentals¶

    Q: What is Salesforce Sales Cloud and what are its core capabilities?¶

    A: Sales Cloud is Salesforce's flagship product designed to help sales teams manage the entire sales process from lead generation to closing deals and beyond. It provides tools for managing customer relationships, tracking sales activities, and forecasting revenue.

    Core Capabilities:

    • Lead Management: Capture, qualify, and convert leads into opportunities
    • Opportunity Management: Track deals through the sales pipeline
    • Account & Contact Management: Maintain customer relationship data
    • Activity Management: Log calls, emails, meetings, and tasks
    • Sales Forecasting: Predict revenue and track quota attainment
    • Reports & Dashboards: Visualize sales performance and metrics
    • Sales Collaboration: Enable team selling with Chatter and Slack integration
    • Mobile Access: Manage sales activities on-the-go

    Key Objects in Sales Cloud:

    • Lead: Potential customer who hasn't been qualified yet
    • Account: Company or organization you do business with
    • Contact: Individual person associated with an Account
    • Opportunity: Qualified sales deal in your pipeline
    • Quote: Pricing proposal sent to customers
    • Product: Items or services your company sells
    • Price Book: Lists of products with pricing information

    Salesforce Edition Requirements:

    • ✅ Essentials: Basic sales features for up to 10 users
    • ✅ Professional: Complete CRM with workflow and API access
    • ✅ Enterprise: Advanced customization, multiple sales processes
    • ✅ Unlimited: Premium features with unlimited support

    Use Cases:

    • B2B sales teams managing complex sales cycles
    • Inside sales teams with high-velocity lead conversion
    • Field sales representatives tracking customer interactions
    • Sales managers forecasting and managing team performance

    🎯 Lead Management¶

    Q: What is the difference between a Lead and a Contact in Sales Cloud?¶

    A: Understanding the distinction between Leads and Contacts is crucial for proper data management and sales process execution in Salesforce.

    Lead:

    • Definition: Unqualified prospect who has shown interest but not yet vetted
    • Purpose: Track potential customers before qualifying them
    • Status: Not yet determined to be a viable sales opportunity
    • Conversion: Can be converted to Account, Contact, and Opportunity
    • Use Case: Marketing campaigns, trade shows, website inquiries

    Contact:

    • Definition: Qualified person associated with an Account
    • Purpose: Maintain relationships with customers and prospects
    • Status: Already qualified and in your CRM system
    • Relationship: Must be related to an Account
    • Use Case: Existing customers, partners, qualified prospects

    Key Differences:

    • Qualification Status: Leads are unqualified, Contacts are qualified
    • Account Association: Leads standalone, Contacts require Account
    • Data Model: Different objects with separate fields and processes
    • Lifecycle: Leads convert to Contacts, Contacts don't convert
    • Duplicates: Can have same person as both Lead and Contact

    Lead Conversion Process:

    When a Lead is converted, Salesforce automatically:

    • Creates a new Account (or associates with existing)
    • Creates a new Contact
    • Optionally creates an Opportunity
    • Transfers Lead custom fields to corresponding objects
    • Changes Lead status to "Converted"

    Q: How do Lead Assignment Rules work in Sales Cloud?¶

    A: Lead Assignment Rules automatically route incoming leads to the right sales representatives based on predefined criteria, ensuring prompt follow-up and balanced workload distribution.

    Key Components:

    • Assignment Rule: Container that holds multiple rule entries
    • Rule Entry: Specific criteria and assignment logic
    • Queue: Group of users who can work on assigned records
    • User Assignment: Direct assignment to specific user
    • Active Rule: Only one lead assignment rule can be active at a time

    How It Works:

    • Leads are evaluated against rule entries in order
    • First matching criteria triggers assignment
    • If no criteria match, lead goes to default assignee or queue
    • Can be triggered via Web-to-Lead, API, or manual lead creation

    💼 Opportunity Management¶

    Q: What are the stages of the Opportunity Sales Process?¶

    A: The Opportunity Sales Process defines the stages a deal progresses through from initial qualification to close. Understanding and properly configuring these stages is critical for accurate pipeline management and forecasting.

    Standard Opportunity Stages:

    • Prospecting: Initial research and outreach to potential customers
    • Qualification: Assessing if prospect is a good fit (BANT criteria)
    • Needs Analysis: Understanding customer requirements and pain points
    • Value Proposition: Presenting solution that addresses customer needs
    • Proposal/Price Quote: Delivering formal proposal with pricing
    • Negotiation/Review: Finalizing terms, pricing, and contract details
    • Closed Won: Deal successfully closed and contract signed
    • Closed Lost: Deal lost to competitor or no decision

    Probability by Stage:

    • Prospecting: 10% probability of close
    • Qualification: 20% probability
    • Needs Analysis: 40% probability
    • Value Proposition: 60% probability
    • Proposal/Price Quote: 75% probability
    • Negotiation/Review: 90% probability
    • Closed Won: 100% (counted in closed revenue)
    • Closed Lost: 0% (removed from pipeline)

    Q: How does Collaborative Forecasting work in Sales Cloud?¶

    A: Collaborative Forecasting enables sales teams to predict revenue, manage quotas, and track performance against targets through a structured forecasting process with management oversight and adjustments.

    Forecasting Types:

    • Opportunity-based: Forecast based on individual opportunities in pipeline
    • Quota-based: Track progress toward assigned quotas
    • Territory-based: Aggregate forecasts across sales territories
    • Product Family: Forecast by product categories

    Forecast Categories:

    • Pipeline: All open opportunities (not committed yet)
    • Best Case: Opportunities you're likely to close plus pipeline
    • Commit: Opportunities you're confident will close
    • Closed: Deals already won this period
    • Omitted: Opportunities excluded from forecast

    // Integration with ERP for order processing
public class ERPIntegrationService {

    // Create order in ERP when Opportunity closes
    @future(callout=true)
    public static void createERPOrder(Id opportunityId) {

        // Query opportunity and products
        Opportunity opp = [
            SELECT Id, Name, AccountId, Amount,
                   (SELECT Product2.Name, Quantity, UnitPrice 
                    FROM OpportunityLineItems)
            FROM Opportunity
            WHERE Id = :opportunityId
        ];

        // Build ERP order payload
        Map<String, Object> erpOrder = new Map<String, Object>{
            'customerNumber' => opp.Account.ERP_Customer_Number__c,
            'orderDate' => System.today().format(),
            'totalAmount' => opp.Amount,
            'lineItems' => new List<Map<String, Object>>()
        };

        // Add line items
        for (OpportunityLineItem oli : opp.OpportunityLineItems) {
            erpOrder.get('lineItems').add(new Map<String, Object>{
                'productCode' => oli.Product2.Product_Code__c,
                'quantity' => oli.Quantity,
                'unitPrice' => oli.UnitPrice
            });
        }

        // Call ERP API
        HttpRequest req = new HttpRequest();
        req.setEndpoint('callout:ERP_System/api/orders');
        req.setMethod('POST');
        req.setHeader('Content-Type', 'application/json');
        req.setBody(JSON.serialize(erpOrder));

        Http http = new Http();
        HttpResponse res = http.send(req);

        if (res.getStatusCode() == 201) {
            // Success - update Salesforce
            Map<String, Object> response = 
                (Map<String, Object>) JSON.deserializeUntyped(res.getBody());

            opp.ERP_Order_Number__c = (String) response.get('orderNumber');
            opp.ERP_Sync_Status__c = 'Synced';
            update opp;

        } else {
            // Error handling
            opp.ERP_Sync_Status__c = 'Failed';
            opp.ERP_Error_Message__c = res.getBody();
            update opp;
        }
    }
}
