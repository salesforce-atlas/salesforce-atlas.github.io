    Overview¶

    Salesforce CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) and Revenue Cloud represent a comprehensive solution for managing the complete quote-to-cash and revenue lifecycle. These powerful tools enable organizations to automate complex pricing scenarios, streamline quote generation, and ensure compliance with modern revenue recognition standards.

    Key Benefits:

    • Accelerated Sales Cycles: Reduce quote generation time from days to minutes
    • Pricing Accuracy: Eliminate manual pricing errors with automated calculations
    • Revenue Compliance: Built-in support for ASC 606 and IFRS 15 standards
    • Subscription Management: Complete lifecycle management for recurring revenue models
    • Scalability: Handle complex product catalogs and pricing structures

    Market Position:

    CPQ and Revenue Cloud solutions are essential for modern B2B organizations, particularly those with:

    • Complex product configurations
    • Subscription-based business models
    • Multi-tier pricing structures
    • High-volume quoting requirements
    • Compliance and audit needs

    CPQ Core Components¶

    Salesforce CPQ consists of several integrated components that work together to streamline the quoting process from product selection through contract execution.

    Product Configuration¶

    The product configuration engine enables sales teams to build complex product bundles while enforcing business rules and constraints.

    Configuration Capabilities:

    • Product Bundles: Group related products with parent-child relationships
    • Option Constraints: Define dependencies and exclusions between products
    • Configuration Rules: Automate product selection based on business logic
    • Product Features: Organize options into logical feature categories
    • Dynamic Pricing: Adjust pricing based on configuration choices

    Configuration Attributes:

    • Configuration type (Bundle, Option, Component)
    • Quantity rules and constraints
    • Feature categories and grouping
    • Hidden vs. visible options
    • Required vs. optional components

    Pricing & Discounting¶

    A sophisticated pricing engine that handles complex pricing scenarios across multiple dimensions.

    Pricing Mechanisms:

    • Price Books: Organize pricing by market, region, or customer segment
    • Price Rules: Automate pricing calculations based on conditions
    • Volume-Based Pricing: Tier pricing based on quantity thresholds
    • Contracted Pricing: Customer-specific negotiated rates
    • Discount Schedules: Time-based and volume-based discount structures
    • Approval Workflows: Route discounts exceeding thresholds for approval

    Pricing Formula Components:

    • List price and cost price
    • Partner and distributor pricing
    • Markup and margin calculations
    • Currency conversion handling
    • Promotional pricing

    Quote Generation¶

    Create professional, accurate quotes with extensive customization capabilities.

    Quote Features:

    • Quote Templates: Branded, professional document templates
    • Multi-Currency Quotes: Automatic currency conversion and management
    • Quote Line Editor: Intuitive interface for managing quote lines
    • Amendment Quotes: Modify existing agreements seamlessly
    • Renewal Quotes: Automated generation for subscription renewals

    Quote Line Fields:

    • Product details and descriptions
    • Quantity and pricing information
    • Discount amounts and percentages
    • Start and end dates
    • Subscription terms
    • Custom fields for specific requirements

    Guided Selling¶

    Intelligent product recommendations that help sales teams identify the right solutions.

    Guided Selling Components:

    • Product Recommendations: AI-powered suggestions based on context
    • Cross-Sell and Upsell: Identify additional revenue opportunities
    • Configuration Wizard: Step-by-step product selection
    • Product Selection Rules: Filter available products based on criteria
    • Search and Filter: Advanced product catalog navigation

    Revenue Cloud Components¶

    Revenue Cloud extends CPQ capabilities to manage the complete revenue lifecycle, from order fulfillment through revenue recognition and renewal.

    Billing¶

    Comprehensive billing management that supports diverse revenue models.

    Billing Capabilities:

    • Invoice Generation: Automated invoice creation from orders
    • Payment Processing: Integration with payment gateways
    • Billing Schedules: Flexible billing frequency and timing
    • Usage-Based Billing: Meter and bill actual consumption
    • Credit and Collections: Manage payments and outstanding balances

    Billing Schedules:

    • In advance vs. in arrears
    • Monthly, quarterly, annual frequencies
    • Custom billing dates
    • Proration calculations
    • Billing alignment options

    Revenue Recognition¶

    Ensure compliance with modern revenue recognition standards while automating complex calculations.

    Revenue Recognition Features:

    • ASC 606 / IFRS 15 Compliance: Built-in support for accounting standards
    • Revenue Scheduling: Automate revenue recognition over time
    • Performance Obligations: Track and manage contract obligations
    • Contract Modifications: Handle amendments and changes
    • Forecasting: Revenue projections and reporting

    Recognition Methods:

    • Straight-line recognition
    • Milestone-based recognition
    • Percentage of completion
    • Deliverable-based recognition
    • Custom recognition rules

    Subscription Management¶

    Complete lifecycle management for subscription-based business models.

    Subscription Features:

    • Subscription Lifecycle: Manage from creation through renewal
    • Amendments and Renewals: Mid-term changes and contract extensions
    • Evergreen Subscriptions: Auto-renewing contracts
    • Co-termination: Align subscription end dates
    • Proration: Calculate charges for partial periods

    Subscription Actions:

    • Add products mid-term
    • Remove products
    • Upgrade or downgrade
    • Change quantities
    • Modify pricing
    • Extend or reduce terms

    Key Objects & Data Model¶

    Understanding the CPQ and Revenue Cloud object model is essential for effective implementation and customization.

    CPQ Objects¶

    Core CPQ Objects:

    • Product2: Standard Salesforce product catalog items
    • Product Code, Name, Description
    • Product family and category
    • Pricing methodology

    • Configuration attributes

    • Price Book Entry: Defines pricing for products in specific price books

    • List price and active status
    • Price book association
    • Currency and unit of measure

    • Use standard price flag

    • Quote: Customer proposal document

    • Account and opportunity linkage
    • Primary contact
    • Expiration date
    • Status and stage

    • Terms and conditions

    • Quote Line: Individual line items within quotes

    • Product reference
    • Quantity and pricing
    • Discounts and net price
    • Start and end dates

    • Subscription details

    • Contracted Price: Special negotiated pricing agreements

    • Account-specific pricing
    • Product association
    • Price and discount information
    • Effective dates
    • Filter criteria

    Revenue Cloud Objects¶

    Revenue Management Objects:

    • Order: Confirmed purchase order
    • Account and contract reference
    • Order date and status
    • Billing account
    • Payment terms

    • Shipping information

    • Order Product: Line items on orders

    • Product and pricing details
    • Quantity ordered
    • Service dates
    • Asset association

    • Revenue recognition schedule

    • Subscription: Recurring service or product

    • Subscription term
    • Renewal model
    • Billing frequency
    • Contract reference

    • Subscription lines

    • Asset: Purchased product or service

    • Install date and location
    • Quantity and serial number
    • Lifecycle status
    • Usage tracking

    • Related subscriptions

    • Invoice: Billing document

    • Invoice date and number
    • Account and billing account
    • Invoice amount
    • Due date and payment status
    • Invoice lines

    Object Relationships¶

    Key Relationship Patterns:

    • Opportunity → Quote (1:Many)
    • Quote → Quote Line (1:Many)
    • Quote → Order (1:1 or 1:Many)
    • Order → Order Product (1:Many)
    • Order Product → Asset (1:1)
    • Order Product → Subscription (1:1)
    • Account → Contracted Price (1:Many)
    • Invoice → Invoice Line (1:Many)

    Automation & Rules Engine¶

    CPQ's powerful rules engine enables sophisticated automation without custom code.

    Product Rules¶

    Control product availability, configuration, and user experience.

    Product Rule Types:

    • Validation Rules: Enforce configuration requirements
    • Error conditions that prevent saving
    • Custom error messages
    • Complex logic conditions

    • Field-level validation

    • Selection Rules: Auto-select or remove products

    • Conditional product addition
    • Dynamic product removal
    • Quantity adjustments

    • Option selection automation

    • Alert Rules: Provide warnings to users

    • Non-blocking notifications
    • Informational messages
    • Best practice guidance

    • Configuration recommendations

    • Filter Rules: Control product visibility

    • Dynamic catalog filtering
    • Context-based availability
    • Search result filtering

    • Option visibility control

    • Configuration Rules: Advanced configuration logic

    • Multi-step configuration flows
    • Complex dependency management
    • Dynamic attribute updates
    • Conditional field display

    Price Rules¶

    Automate complex pricing calculations.

    Price Rule Components:

    • Price Conditions: Define when rules execute
    • Quote-level conditions
    • Product-level conditions
    • Configuration attributes
    • Custom field values

    • Date and time conditions

    • Price Actions: Define pricing calculations

    • Set price values
    • Apply discounts
    • Calculate surcharges
    • Update custom fields

    • Execute formula calculations

    • Summary Variables: Aggregate calculations

    • Sum quantities across lines
    • Calculate totals by category
    • Count products matching criteria
    • Find minimum or maximum values

    • Average calculations

    • Quote Calculators: Custom calculation plugins

    • JavaScript-based calculations
    • Complex business logic
    • External system integration
    • Custom pricing algorithms

    Price Rule Evaluation:

    • Evaluation event (Before Calculate, Before Save, After Calculate)
    • Evaluation scope (Quote, Quote Line, Configuration)
    • Execution order and prioritization
    • Rule chaining and dependencies

    • Use product families consistently
    • Implement clear naming conventions
    • Document product relationships

    • Maintain catalog cleanliness

    • Configuration Planning: Design bundles and options strategically

    • Minimize configuration complexity
    • Use features to organize options
    • Implement appropriate constraints

    • Test configurations thoroughly

    • Attribute Management: Standardize product attributes

    • Define required vs. optional attributes
    • Use picklists for consistency
    • Document attribute purposes
    • Maintain data quality

    Pricing Strategy¶

    • Monitor rule performance

    Key Features Used:

    • Subscription pricing
    • Amendment quotes
    • Renewal automation
    • Evergreen subscriptions
    • Co-termination

    Scenario: Technology hardware with software and services

    Implementation:

    • Hardware product as bundle parent
