    Certified Technical Architect (CTA) Overview¶

    What is a Salesforce CTA?¶

    The Salesforce Certified Technical Architect (CTA) is the highest technical certification in the Salesforce ecosystem. CTAs are elite professionals who design and deliver complex, enterprise-scale Salesforce solutions.

    Certification Path¶

    Prerequisites¶

    1. Active Salesforce Certifications (minimum):
    2. Application Architect
    3. System Architect

    4. At least one additional certification

    5. Recommended Experience:

    6. 3-5+ years of Salesforce experience
    7. Multiple implementation projects
    8. Cross-cloud experience
    9. Leadership and mentoring experience

    Review Board Format¶

    The Challenge¶

    • Duration: 3 hours total
    • Part 1: Scenario analysis (75 minutes)
    • Part 2: Presentation preparation (30 minutes)
    • Part 3: Presentation and Q&A (75 minutes)

    Evaluation Criteria¶

    1. Solution Design (30%)
    2. Communication (25%)
    3. Depth of Knowledge (25%)
    4. Leadership (20%)

    Success Strategies¶

    1. Practice Scenarios: Work through as many as possible
    2. Whiteboarding: Practice diagramming solutions
    3. Peer Review: Join study groups and practice presentations
    4. Stay Current: Keep up with Salesforce releases
    5. Real-World Experience: Apply learnings to actual projects
    6. Mock Reviews: Attend or organize practice review boards
    7. Document Patterns: Build your own solution pattern library
    8. Time Management: Practice within time constraints

    Common Pitfalls to Avoid¶

    • Over-engineering solutions
    • Ignoring non-technical requirements
    • Poor time management
    • Weak communication
    • Lack of justification for decisions
    • Ignoring governor limits and scalability
    • Not considering maintenance and support

    • Study Time: 6-12 months of focused preparation
