    Review Board Preparation¶

    Review Board Overview¶

    Format & Structure¶

    Timeline¶

    • Total Duration: ~3 hours
    • Scenario Analysis: 75 minutes
    • Preparation: 30 minutes
    • Presentation: 15-20 minutes
    • Q&A/Defense: 55-60 minutes

    Panel Composition¶

    • 5-6 Salesforce CTAs
    • Diverse expertise (technical, industry, clouds)
    • Global representation

    Evaluation Criteria¶

    Solution Design (30%)¶

    • Architecture quality
    • Scalability
    • Best practices
    • Trade-off decisions
    • Completeness

    Communication (25%)¶

    • Clarity of presentation
    • Whiteboarding skills
    • Handling questions
    • Professional demeanor

    Depth of Knowledge (25%)¶

    • Technical expertise
    • Platform understanding
    • Industry knowledge
    • Breadth across clouds

    Leadership (20%)¶

    • Strategic thinking
    • Decision-making
    • Risk assessment
    • Stakeholder management

    Scenario Analysis Phase (75 minutes)¶

    What to Expect¶

    Scenario Packet¶

    • Business context and requirements
    • Current state description
    • Constraints and assumptions
    • Stakeholder information
    • Technical environment
    • Success criteria

    Analysis Focus Areas¶

    1. Business Requirements
    2. Identify key requirements
    3. Prioritize must-haves vs. nice-to-haves

    4. Note ambiguities for clarification

    5. Technical Requirements

    6. Data model needs
    7. Integration points
    8. Security requirements

    9. Performance needs

    10. Constraints

    11. Budget limitations
    12. Timeline constraints
    13. Resource availability

    14. Technical limitations

    15. Assumptions

    16. What's explicitly stated
    17. What's implied

    18. What needs clarification

    19. Risks

    20. Technical risks
    21. Business risks
    22. Mitigation strategies

    Analysis Strategy¶

    Read Through (15 minutes)¶

    • First pass: Overall understanding
    • Highlight key points
    • Note questions

    Deep Dive (30 minutes)¶

    • Identify stakeholders
    • Map requirements
    • Data model sketch
    • Integration points
    • Security model

    Solution Design (20 minutes)¶

    • High-level architecture
    • Key design decisions
    • Trade-offs
    • Risks and mitigations

    Final Review (10 minutes)¶

    • Completeness check
    • Key messages
    • Anticipated questions

    Presentation Structure¶

    Recommended Flow¶

    1. Introduction (2 minutes)
    2. Understanding of business
    3. Key requirements

    4. Success criteria

    5. Solution Architecture (8 minutes)

    6. High-level architecture
    7. Data model
    8. Integration approach

    9. Security model

    10. Key Design Decisions (3 minutes)

    11. Critical choices made
    12. Trade-offs considered

    13. Rationale

    14. Risks & Mitigations (2 minutes)

    15. Identified risks
    16. Mitigation strategies

    17. Contingency plans

    18. Conclusion (< 1 minute)

    19. Summary
    20. Open for questions

    Key Messages¶

    • Identify 3-5 key messages
    • Reinforce throughout
    • Tie to business value
    • Support with evidence

    Presentation Phase (15-20 minutes)¶

    Delivery Tips¶

    Start Strong¶

    • Confident introduction
    • Show you understand the business
    • Set the context
    • Outline your approach

    Whiteboard Technique¶

    • Face the panel (not the board)
    • Explain as you draw
    • Point and reference
    • Use consistent notation
    • Don't block the view

    Pacing¶

    • Not too fast (nervous energy)
    • Not too slow (lose attention)
    • Pause for emphasis
    • Check for understanding

    Body Language¶

    • Confident posture
    • Eye contact with panel
    • Natural gestures
    • Avoid fidgeting
    • Move purposefully

    Common Mistakes¶

    ❌ Avoid: - Reading from whiteboard - Too much detail - Ignoring requirements - Over-architecting - Defensive tone - Dismissing questions

    ✅ Do: - Engage with panel - Right level of detail - Address all requirements - Right-sized solution - Welcome questions - Acknowledge uncertainty

    Q&A / Defense Phase (55-60 minutes)¶

    Question Types¶

    Clarification Questions¶

    • "Can you explain...?"
    • "What did you mean by...?"
    • "Walk us through..."

    Response Strategy: - Clarify succinctly - Use whiteboard to illustrate - Check for understanding

    Probing Questions¶

    • "Why did you choose...?"
    • "Have you considered...?"
    • "What if...?"

    Response Strategy: - Explain rationale - Acknowledge alternatives - Discuss trade-offs - Be open to feedback

    Challenge Questions¶

    • "This won't work because..."
    • "You missed...?"
    • "What about...?"

    Response Strategy: - Listen fully - Don't be defensive - Consider the point - Adjust if needed - Explain reasoning

    Scenario Extensions¶

    • "What if the requirement changes to...?"
    • "How would you handle...?"
    • "The business now wants...?"

    Response Strategy: - Think through impact - Identify what changes - Propose adjustments - Discuss implications

    Handling Difficult Questions¶

    "I Don't Know"¶

    • It's okay to not know everything
    • Don't make up answers
    • Say how you'd find out
    • Show problem-solving approach

    Multiple Panel Members Asking¶

    • Address questioner by name
    • One question at a time
    • "Let me address that, then I'll come back to your question"

    Disagreement with Panel¶

    • Listen to their perspective
    • Acknowledge the point
    • Explain your reasoning
    • Be willing to adjust
    • Don't be defensive

    Time Management¶

    • Monitor time
    • Prioritize questions
    • Concise answers
    • Offer to go deeper if time

    Preparation Strategies¶

    Study Approach¶

    6-12 Month Plan¶

    • Months 1-3: Foundation certifications
    • Months 4-6: Architect certifications
    • Months 7-9: Scenario practice
    • Months 10-12: Mock reviews

    Weekly Study¶

    • Study groups
    • Practice scenarios
    • Whiteboarding
    • Read scenario reviews
    • Stay current with releases

    • Real project experience

    Practice Strategy¶

    • Timed practice (75 minutes)
    • Solo analysis first
    • Group discussion
    • Mock presentations
    • Peer feedback

    Mock Review Boards¶

    • Practice presentation
    • Get feedback
    • Identify gaps
    • Build confidence

    Find Mock Reviews¶

    • CTA study groups
    • Salesforce community
    • Paid services
    • Internal company practice

    Whiteboarding Practice¶

    Regular Practice¶

    • Practice drawing architectures
    • Consistent notation
    • Speed and clarity
    • Different scenarios
    • Teach others

    Feedback¶

    • Record yourself
    • Peer review
    • Improve over time

    Day-Of Tips¶

    Before the Review¶

    Mental Preparation¶

    • Good night's sleep
    • Arrive early
    • Calm and focused
    • Positive mindset
    • Confidence in preparation

    Physical Preparation¶

    • Professional attire
    • Water available
    • Markers/whiteboard check
    • Comfortable temperature
    • Minimize distractions

    During the Review¶

    Analysis Phase¶

    • Manage time (check clock)
    • Don't panic if stuck
    • Make progress, not perfection
    • Note questions for later

    Presentation¶

    • Breathe
    • Smile
    • Engage with panel
    • Show enthusiasm
    • Be yourself

    Q&A¶

    • Listen carefully
    • Pause before answering
    • It's okay to think
    • Ask for clarification
    • Stay composed

    After the Review¶

    Reflection¶

    • Note what went well
    • Areas for improvement
    • Questions asked
    • Learn from experience

    Results¶

    • Notified in 2-3 weeks
    • Pass or fail (no scores)
    • Feedback provided (if fail)
    • Can reapply after 30 days

    If You Don't Pass¶

    It's Common¶

    • Many CTAs don't pass first time
    • Average 2-3 attempts
    • Learn from feedback
    • Adjust preparation
    • Try again

    Feedback Analysis¶

    • What areas to improve?
    • Practice those areas
    • Seek additional help
    • Mock reviews
    • Reapply when ready

    Success Factors¶

    Technical Skills¶

    • Deep Salesforce knowledge
    • Multi-cloud experience
    • Integration expertise
    • Architecture patterns
    • Best practices

    Soft Skills¶

    • Communication clarity
    • Whiteboarding ability
    • Handling pressure
    • Confidence
    • Humility

    Preparation¶

    • Sufficient study time
    • Practice scenarios
    • Mock reviews
    • Whiteboarding practice
    • Current with platform

    Mindset¶

    • Growth mindset
    • Learn from failures
    • Persistent
    • Confident but humble
    • Genuine passion

    Common Pitfalls¶

    Over-Engineering¶

    • Unnecessarily complex
    • Too many components
    • Gold-plating
    • Solution: Right-size for requirements

    Under-Explaining¶

    • Assumptions without explanation
    • Skipping rationale
    • Too high-level
    • Solution: Explain your thinking

    Ignoring Requirements¶

    • Missing key requirements
    • Misinterpreting scenario
    • Focusing on wrong things
    • Solution: Map solution to requirements

    Poor Time Management¶

    • Rushing presentation
    • Not finishing analysis
    • Unbalanced coverage
    • Solution: Practice with timer

    Defensive Behavior¶

    • Arguing with panel
    • Dismissing feedback
    • Rigid thinking
    • Solution: Open mindset, collaborative

    1. Be Yourself: Authentic beats perfect
    2. Show Your Work: Explain your thinking
