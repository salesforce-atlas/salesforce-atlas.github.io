    Leadership & Project Management¶

    Leadership Fundamentals¶

    Leadership vs. Management¶

    Leadership¶

    • Set vision and direction
    • Inspire and motivate
    • Drive change
    • Long-term focus
    • Influence

    Management¶

    • Plan and organize
    • Execute and control
    • Maintain stability
    • Short-term focus
    • Authority

    Leadership Styles¶

    Transformational¶

    • Inspire vision
    • Motivate change
    • Develop people
    • Innovation-focused

    Servant Leadership¶

    • Support team first
    • Remove obstacles
    • Empowerment
    • Team success focus

    Situational Leadership¶

    • Adapt to context
    • Assess team maturity
    • Flex approach
    • Task and relationship balance

    Team Leadership¶

    Building High-Performing Teams¶

    Team Stages (Tuckman)¶

    1. Forming: Getting to know each other
    2. Storming: Conflict and competition
    3. Norming: Cooperation and cohesion
    4. Performing: High productivity
    5. Adjourning: Project completion

    Team Dynamics¶

    • Clear roles and responsibilities
    • Psychological safety
    • Mutual trust
    • Shared goals
    • Open communication

    Motivation¶

    Intrinsic Motivators¶

    • Autonomy
    • Mastery
    • Purpose
    • Recognition
    • Growth

    Extrinsic Motivators¶

    • Compensation
    • Bonuses
    • Promotions
    • Perks

    Delegation¶

    • Match task to skill
    • Provide context
    • Empower decision-making
    • Monitor progress
    • Give feedback

    Phases¶

    1. Requirements
    2. Design
    3. Implementation
    4. Testing
    5. Deployment
    6. Maintenance

    When to Use¶

    • Fixed requirements
    • Regulatory compliance
    • Large, complex projects
    • Distributed teams

    Hybrid Approach¶

    • Waterfall for planning
    • Agile for execution
    • Balance structure and flexibility

    Risk Response Strategies¶

    • Avoid: Eliminate the risk
    • Mitigate: Reduce probability/impact
    • Transfer: Shift to third party
    • Accept: Acknowledge and monitor

    Innovation & Continuous Improvement¶

    Innovation Culture¶

    • Encourage experimentation
    • Accept failure
    • Reward ideas
    • Collaborate
    • Learn continuously

    Continuous Improvement¶

    • Retrospectives
    • Lessons learned
    • Process optimization

    • Best practice sharing

    Kaizen Approach¶

    • Small, incremental changes
    • Everyone involved
    • Continuous effort
    • Sustainable improvement

    Best Practices¶

    1. Lead by Example: Model desired behaviors
    2. Clear Vision: Articulate direction
    3. Empower Team: Trust and delegate
    4. Communicate: Frequently and transparently
    5. Celebrate Wins: Recognize achievements
    6. Learn from Failures: Growth mindset
    7. Adapt: Flexible approach
    8. Build Relationships: Invest in people
    9. Deliver Value: Focus on outcomes
    10. Continuous Learning: Stay current

    Common Challenges¶

    Challenge: Scope Creep¶

    Solution: Change control, clear requirements, stakeholder management

    Challenge: Resource Constraints¶

    Solution: Prioritization, creative solutions, escalation

    Challenge: Team Conflicts¶

    Solution: Address early, mediate, focus on goals

    Challenge: Stakeholder Alignment¶

    Solution: Regular communication, manage expectations, involve early
