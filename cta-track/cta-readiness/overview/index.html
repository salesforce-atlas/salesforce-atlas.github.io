    CTA Readiness Overview¶

    Final Preparation for Review Board Success¶

    You've built your knowledge, practiced your skills, and developed your professional competencies. Now it's time to bring everything together and prepare for the CTA Review Board.

    What is CTA Readiness?¶

    CTA Readiness is the final phase where you: - Integrate all learned skills into cohesive performance - Practice full review board simulations - Refine your presentation and defense techniques - Build confidence for the actual review - Address any remaining knowledge gaps

    Panel Composition¶

    • 5-6 Salesforce CTAs
    • Diverse expertise (technical, industry, multiple clouds)
    • Global representation
    • Evaluating you against CTA standards

    Evaluation Criteria¶

    The panel assesses you on:

    Solution Design (30%) - Meets all requirements - Scalable and performant - Follows best practices - Well-architected

    Communication (25%) - Clear presentation - Effective whiteboarding - Confident delivery - Handles questions well

    Depth of Knowledge (25%) - Multi-cloud expertise - Integration knowledge - Security and governance - Platform understanding

    Leadership (20%) - Strategic thinking - Decision-making - Risk assessment - Stakeholder awareness

    Readiness Self-Assessment¶

    Rate yourself honestly (1-5 scale):

    Technical Depth - [ ] Can design across 3+ clouds confidently (Target: 5) - [ ] Deep expertise in 2+ specialty areas (Target: 5) - [ ] Understand integration patterns thoroughly (Target: 5) - [ ] Current with latest 2 Salesforce releases (Target: 4)

    Scenario Analysis - [ ] Complete 75-min scenario in time (Target: 5) - [ ] Identify all key requirements (Target: 5) - [ ] Design complete solution (Target: 5) - [ ] Document trade-offs and risks (Target: 5)

    Whiteboarding - [ ] Draw clear architecture diagrams (Target: 5) - [ ] Explain while drawing (Target: 5) - [ ] Consistent notation (Target: 5) - [ ] Professional presentation (Target: 5)

    Defense & Q&A - [ ] Handle probing questions confidently (Target: 4) - [ ] Defend decisions with rationale (Target: 5) - [ ] Admit gaps gracefully (Target: 4) - [ ] Adjust based on feedback (Target: 4)

    Communication - [ ] Executive-level presentations (Target: 4) - [ ] Adapt to audience (Target: 4) - [ ] Concise explanations (Target: 5) - [ ] Professional demeanor (Target: 5)

    Target: All areas at 4+ before scheduling review board

    Final Preparation Timeline¶

    8 Weeks Before Review Board¶

    Focus: Mock reviews and scenario practice

    • Week 1-2: Full mock review boards (2-3 sessions)
    • Week 3-4: Scenario-based question practice
    • Week 5-6: Whiteboarding and presentation refinement
    • Week 7-8: Knowledge gap closure and confidence building

    4 Weeks Before¶

    Focus: Intensive practice

    • Daily scenario analysis (30-45 min)
    • Weekly mock presentations to study group
    • Peer feedback and iteration
    • Review weak areas identified in mocks

    2 Weeks Before¶

    Focus: Fine-tuning

    • Light review of key concepts
    • Whiteboarding practice daily
    • Mock Q&A sessions
    • Mental preparation

    1 Week Before¶

    Focus: Rest and confidence

    • No new learning (trust your preparation)
    • Light scenario review
    • Visualization exercises
    • Good sleep and self-care

    Day Before¶

    Focus: Relax

    • Review high-level concepts only
    • Prepare materials (markers, water)
    • Get good night's sleep
    • Positive mindset

    Mock Review Board Practice¶

    Finding Mock Review Opportunities¶

    CTA Study Groups: Many organize mock reviews Paid Services: Professional mock review services Internal Practice: Colleagues role-play as panel Peer Practice: Study group members take turns

    Mock Review Checklist¶

    Setup: - [ ] Full 3-hour time block - [ ] Whiteboard ready - [ ] 5-6 "panel members" recruited - [ ] Scenario prepared (not seen before) - [ ] Recording device (optional but helpful)

    During Mock: - [ ] Treat as real review board - [ ] Manage time strictly - [ ] Present confidently - [ ] Handle challenges professionally

    After Mock: - [ ] Receive detailed feedback - [ ] Note strength areas - [ ] Identify improvement areas - [ ] Create action plan - [ ] Schedule follow-up mock

    Common Mock Review Feedback¶

    Feedback: "Too much detail on standard features" Action: Focus on custom/complex parts, mention standard features briefly

    Feedback: "Didn't defend trade-offs well" Action: Practice explaining alternatives and why you chose your approach

    Feedback: "Ran out of time during presentation" Action: Practice 15-minute presentations, prioritize key points

    Feedback: "Defensive when challenged" Action: Practice staying calm, acknowledging points, explaining rationale

    Mental Preparation¶

    Building Confidence¶

    Preparation Breeds Confidence: - You've studied for months - You've done mock reviews - You know your stuff - Trust your preparation

    Positive Self-Talk: - "I am well-prepared for this" - "I can handle any question" - "This is my opportunity to demonstrate my expertise" - "I belong here"

    Managing Anxiety¶

    Physical Techniques: - Deep breathing (4-7-8 technique) - Progressive muscle relaxation - Exercise day before - Good sleep

    Mental Techniques: - Visualization (see yourself succeeding) - Reframe anxiety as excitement - Focus on what you control - Remember: It's a conversation, not an interrogation

    Day-Of Mindset¶

    Morning Routine: - Light breakfast - Review high-level concepts only - Positive affirmations - Arrive 15 minutes early

    During Review: - Take deep breaths - Smile and be personable - Listen carefully to questions - It's okay to pause and think

    Success Stories & Lessons Learned¶

    Common Themes from Successful CTAs¶

    "I didn't know everything, and that was okay" - Panel expects you to know a lot, not everything - Admitting gaps with plan to find out is respected

    "The panel wanted me to succeed" - They're evaluating, not trying to fail you - They want more CTAs in the community

    "Practice made perfect" - Those who did many mocks performed better - Whiteboarding practice was invaluable

    "Stay calm and think out loud" - Showing your thinking process is valuable - It's okay to pause and think

    If You Don't Pass¶

    It Happens to Many: - Average 2-3 attempts to pass - Many successful CTAs didn't pass first time - It's a learning experience

    What to Do: 1. Review feedback carefully: What areas to improve? 2. Create improvement plan: Target weak areas 3. Practice more: Mock reviews, scenarios 4. Reapply when ready: 30-day wait period 5. Stay positive: You're closer than before

    Final Checklist¶

    Technical Readiness¶

    • [ ] Can design across Sales, Service, Marketing, CPQ clouds
    • [ ] Deep knowledge of integration patterns
    • [ ] Security and data governance expertise
    • [ ] Current with recent Salesforce releases

    Scenario Analysis¶

    • [ ] Completed 20+ practice scenarios
    • [ ] Can finish 75-min scenario in time
    • [ ] Identify all requirements consistently
    • [ ] Design complete solutions

    Presentation Skills¶

    • [ ] Whiteboarding practice (50+ hours)
    • [ ] Presented to study groups (10+ times)
    • [ ] Comfortable with 15-minute presentations
    • [ ] Professional delivery

    Defense & Q&A¶

    • [ ] Mock review board practice (3+ sessions)
    • [ ] Comfortable handling challenges
    • [ ] Can think on feet
    • [ ] Gracefully admit gaps

    Mental Readiness¶

    • [ ] Confident in preparation
    • [ ] Anxiety managed
    • [ ] Positive mindset
    • [ ] Ready to demonstrate expertise
