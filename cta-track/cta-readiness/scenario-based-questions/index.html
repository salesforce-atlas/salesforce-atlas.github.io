    Scenario-Based Questions¶

    CTA-Style Practice Questions¶

    This guide provides scenario-based questions across different domains to help you practice for the CTA review board.

    How to Use This Guide¶

    1. Read the scenario carefully (2-3 minutes)
    2. Analyze requirements - identify key points
    3. Design solution - sketch architecture
    4. Prepare answer - structure your response
    5. Time yourself - practice under pressure
    6. Review - check completeness

    Data Architecture Scenarios¶

    Scenario 1: Complex Account Hierarchy¶

    Business Context: Global manufacturing company with complex account relationships:

    • Corporate headquarters
    • Regional offices
    • Manufacturing plants
    • Distribution centers
    • Retail locations

    Requirements: - Track 5-level account hierarchy - Roll-up revenue across hierarchy - Different sales processes per account type - Regional data visibility rules - Historical hierarchy tracking

    Questions to Answer: 1. How would you model the account hierarchy? 2. What object relationships would you use? 3. How would you handle revenue roll-ups? 4. What's your approach to historical tracking? 5. How do you ensure data integrity?

    Key Considerations: - Master-Detail vs Lookup relationships - Roll-up summary limitations (5 levels deep) - Sharing model for regional visibility - Performance with large hierarchy queries

    Scenario 2: Multi-Currency Opportunity Management¶

    Business Context: Software company selling in 50+ countries with complex pricing:

    • Multi-currency opportunities
    • Historical exchange rates for reporting
    • Subscription with annual escalation clauses
    • Partner/direct sales channels

    Requirements: - Support 25+ currencies - Track exchange rates historically - Revenue recognition over time - Pipeline reporting in corporate currency - Partner commission calculations

    Questions: 1. How do you handle multi-currency requirements? 2. What's your approach to historical exchange rates? 3. How do you support subscription revenue recognition? 4. How do you calculate commissions across currencies?

    Key Considerations: - Advanced Currency Management vs dated exchange rates - Revenue Cloud for complex revenue recognition - Custom objects for historical rate tracking - Reporting and dashboard currency handling

    Integration Scenarios¶

    Scenario 3: Real-Time Order Processing¶

    Business Context: E-commerce company needs real-time order sync between website and Salesforce:

    • 50,000 orders/day (peaks at 100,000)
    • Real-time inventory checks
    • Order status updates to customers
    • Integration with warehouse system

    Requirements: - Near real-time order creation in Salesforce - Inventory availability checks (<2 sec response) - Order status updates propagate to all systems - Error handling and retry logic - Monitor and alert on failures

    Questions: 1. What integration pattern would you use? 2. How do you handle peak load of 100,000 orders/day? 3. What's your error handling strategy? 4. How do you ensure data consistency? 5. What technology stack do you recommend?

    Key Considerations: - Platform Events for async processing - API rate limits and bulk operations - Queueable Apex for chaining - External caching for inventory - MuleSoft vs custom integration

    Scenario 4: Legacy System Migration¶

    Business Context: Insurance company migrating from 30-year-old mainframe to Salesforce:

    • 10 million policy records
    • Complex business rules in COBOL
    • Must maintain business continuity
    • Regulatory compliance requirements

    Requirements: - Phased migration approach - Co-existence period (6 months) - Data validation and reconciliation - Zero data loss tolerance - Compliance with insurance regulations

    Questions: 1. What migration strategy do you recommend? 2. How do you ensure data quality? 3. What's your approach to business rule migration? 4. How do you handle the co-existence period? 5. How do you validate migration success?

    Key Considerations: - Strangler pattern for gradual migration - Data mapping and transformation - Bi-directional sync during transition - Automated testing and validation - Regulatory audit requirements

    Security & Compliance Scenarios¶

    Scenario 5: GDPR Compliance¶

    Business Context: Marketing company operating in EU needs GDPR compliance:

    • Handle personal data of EU citizens
    • Right to be forgotten
    • Data portability
    • Consent management

    Requirements: - Identify and classify personal data - Implement consent management - Support data subject requests - Audit trail of data access - Data retention policies

    Questions: 1. How do you identify personal data in Salesforce? 2. What's your consent management strategy? 3. How do you implement "right to be forgotten"? 4. How do you support data portability? 5. What audit mechanisms do you implement?

    Key Considerations: - Shield Platform Encryption for data at rest - Custom objects for consent tracking - Automated data deletion workflows - Data export capabilities - Event Monitoring for audit

    Solution Design Scenarios¶

    Scenario 6: Omni-Channel Contact Center¶

    Business Context: Telecommunications company implementing omni-channel support:

    • Phone, email, chat, SMS, social media
    • 1,000 agents across 3 regions
    • AI-powered routing and deflection
    • 24/7 operations

    Requirements: - Unified agent console - Intelligent work routing - Knowledge deflection - Real-time reporting - Integration with telephony

    Questions: 1. What Service Cloud features would you use? 2. How do you design the routing strategy? 3. What's your knowledge management approach? 4. How do you integrate with telephony? 5. How do you ensure 24/7 operations?

    Key Considerations: - Omni-Channel for work routing - Service Cloud Voice vs CTI - Einstein for deflection and classification - Knowledge with AI-powered recommendations - High availability architecture

    Scenario 7: CPQ with Complex Pricing¶

    Business Context: Manufacturing company with complex B2B pricing:

    • 5,000+ SKUs with bundles
    • Volume and loyalty discounting
    • Multi-year contracts with escalation
    • Partner and direct channels

    Requirements: - Configure complex product bundles - Dynamic pricing based on multiple factors - 6-level approval process - Integration with ERP for fulfillment - Contract amendments and renewals

    Questions: 1. How do you structure the product catalog? 2. What's your pricing and discounting strategy? 3. How do you design the approval workflow? 4. How do you handle contract amendments? 5. What's your integration approach with ERP?

    Key Considerations: - CPQ product rules and price rules - Advanced approval with Flow Orchestration - Salesforce Billing vs external billing - Contract lifecycle management - Order to Cash integration

    Practice Defense Questions¶

    For each scenario, prepare to answer:

    Clarifying Questions¶

    • "What assumptions did you make?"
    • "Can you clarify what you meant by X?"
    • "Walk me through the data flow"

    Probing Questions¶

    • "Why did you choose X over Y?"
    • "What are the trade-offs of this approach?"
    • "How does this scale?"

    Challenge Questions¶

    • "This won't work because..."
    • "Have you considered Z?"
    • "What if the requirement changes to..."

    Technical Deep-Dive¶

    • "Explain the technical implementation"
    • "How do you handle errors?"
    • "What governor limits are you concerned about?"

    Practice Tips¶

    1. Time yourself: Answer each scenario in 15 minutes
    2. Whiteboard: Draw your solution, don't just think it
    3. Explain out loud: Practice articulating your reasoning
    4. Record yourself: Review and improve
    5. Study group: Present to peers and get feedback

    Answer Framework¶

    Use this structure for every scenario:

    1. Restate: "I understand the requirement is..."
    2. Clarify: "My assumptions are..."
    3. Solution: "My recommended approach is..."
    4. Rationale: "I chose this because..."
    5. Trade-offs: "The trade-offs are..."
    6. Alternatives: "I also considered..."
    7. Risks: "The risks are... mitigated by..."

    Practice these scenarios until you can confidently design and defend solutions!
